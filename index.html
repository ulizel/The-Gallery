Tips for saving
  • iPhone: in the save sheet, tap “Save N Images”. Photos files them by the date stored inside each image.
  • Android: pick your gallery or Google Photos in the share sheet. If your gallery shows downloaded files under today, share them straight into the gallery app instead.
  • No share sheet? Download the ZIP, unzip it in Files, then select the images and save. Tapping a preview also opens the image so you can long-press to save.
  • Your settings are remembered on this device. Your real photos are never touched.
