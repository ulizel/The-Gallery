Tips for saving
  • iPhone: tap “Save to Photos / Share”, then “Save N Images”. Photos files them by the date stored inside each image.
  • Android: use “Save to Photos / Share” and pick your gallery or Google Photos. If you download instead and your gallery shows them under today, share them straight into the gallery app.
  • No share sheet? Download the ZIP, unzip it in Files, then select the images and save. Tapping a preview also opens the image so you can long-press to save.
  • Running it on your phone: on Android, open this file from your file manager in Chrome. On iPhone, local files often don’t run scripts, so upload it to any free static host once (Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages) and use Add to Home Screen.
  • Your settings are remembered on this device. Your real photos are never touched.
